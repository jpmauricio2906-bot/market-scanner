Entry Timing ranks momentum recovery, position within the trend and volume confirmation. Trend measures direction and strength; a bullish trend can still have a weak entry. Exit Pressure measures persistent loss of 50-day support, with a faster exit for severe high-volume breakdowns. Ordinary pullbacks can remain Hold. Exits require a completed daily candle. Confirmed buy/sell adds two closes holding a breakout level and volume confirmation; it does not mean higher expected returns. Composite keeps your weighted indicator view and does not set Buy/Sell signals. Scores run from 0 to 100 and are not probabilities.
